# Demo Script & Screenshot List

## 3-minute live demo (for the viva / presentation)

1. **Setup (before the demo).** `make dev` — backend on :8000, frontend on
   :5173. Good lighting, face centred, plain background.
2. **0:00 – The problem.** Open the Live Monitor, start the camera with
   protection OFF. Point at the "Before" gauge: recognition confidence
   ~98%. "Any frame of this call can be scraped and matched."
3. **0:45 – Turn the cloak on.** Flip the protection switch. The "After"
   gauge falls to under 10% while the two video panels look identical.
   Drag the split-slider across the frame to prove it.
4. **1:30 – Controls.** Raise epsilon 8/255 → 16/255: confidence falls
   further, noise becomes faintly visible. Drop it back. Toggle EOT off:
   note that robustness after compression is the point of EOT.
5. **2:15 – Benchmarks.** Open the Benchmarks page, run the eval
   (or show the pre-run `eval/results.csv`): FGSM vs PGD vs Ours, white-box
   vs black-box, and the robustness columns.
6. **2:45 – Limitations.** Close with the threat model: passive scrapers
   yes, adaptive attackers no. One sentence, no hand-waving.
